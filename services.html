Support Services

Helpful planning services and operating hours for travelers who want more than just an itinerary.

Main support categories

  1. Transportation
    • Airport shuttle booking assistance — Daily, 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
    • Train and bus schedule support — Monday to Sunday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
    • Private car reservation help — Daily, 24-hour request window
  2. Trip Maintenance
    • Same-day itinerary updates — Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
    • Weather-based backup planning — Daily, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
    • Local activity substitutions — Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
  3. Cleaning & Preparation
    • Packing checklist assistance — Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
    • Hotel room preference requests — Daily, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM
    • Post-trip organization tips — Sunday, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM

Additional local support

  1. Dining Assistance
    • Restaurant reservation help — Daily, 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM
    • Dietary accommodation planning — Monday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM
    • Nearby café recommendations — Daily, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  2. Lodging Support
    • Hotel comparison support — Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
    • Late check-in guidance — Daily, 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM
    • Budget stay alternatives — Daily, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
  3. Experience Planning
    • Museum and event suggestions — Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
    • Walking route recommendations — Daily, sunrise to sunset
    • Seasonal attraction updates — Friday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

These support hours are sample business hours designed for the course project. For a custom request, use the validated contact form on the Contact page.
